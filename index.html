01 / About

Who I am

Fiona Tang is a fashion designer working across collection design, beading & embroidery, CLO 3D, print, and AI-assisted exploration.

My practice sits between atelier craft and contemporary digital tools — building seasonal collections with clear silhouette language, refined surface design, and production-ready technical packs.

I am skilled in garment construction thinking, lookbook storytelling, embroidery / beading development, CLO Design, print design, tech packs, and collaborative brand work.
